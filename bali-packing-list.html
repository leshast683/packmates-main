Bali Packing List: What to Actually Pack

Bali runs hot and humid year-round with sudden downpours, and most temples require covered shoulders and knees — pack for sun, water, and one modest outfit.

Bali is warm and humid nearly every month, so this list leans tropical. If you're visiting a temple (Uluwatu, Tanah Lot, Ubud's water temples), bring one outfit that covers your shoulders and knees — sarongs are available to rent on-site, but having your own saves time. Most trips also include at least one rice-terrace or volcano hike, which pulls in its own gear.
