Cancún Packing List: What to Actually Pack

Cancún is hot, humid, and built around the beach and the pool — pack light, quick-dry fabrics and reef-safe sunscreen if you're snorkeling the reef.

Most resorts sell forgotten sunscreen and toiletries at a steep markup, and many now require reef-safe (oxybenzone-free) sunscreen specifically for snorkeling excursions — worth checking the label before you buy.
