Privacy Policy

This explains what information Packmates AI collects, why, and how you're in control of it.

Last updated: July 2026

Who we are

Packmates AI ("we," "us") operates the packmatesai.com website and the Packmates AI mobile app. This policy applies to both.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect the email address and password you provide, or — if you choose "Sign in with Google" — the name, email address, and profile photo Google shares with us for that purpose.

As you use the app, we store the content you create: your profile details (display name, bio, avatar photo, home location if you add one), your trips (destinations, dates, activities, cover photos), your packing lists and packing progress, and who you've connected with as travel "Packmates."

To show weather for a trip, we send that trip's destination coordinates to the Open-Meteo weather service. This is a public weather API that doesn't require an account or receive any information that identifies you.

We use Google Analytics to understand overall usage of the website (e.g. which pages are visited) at an aggregate level. We don't use it to build individual advertising profiles.

If something breaks, technical details about the error (not your personal content) may be logged to help us fix it.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and let you sign in.
  • To provide the core features of the app — trip planning, AI-generated packing lists, weather, and real-time collaboration with people you invite to a trip.
  • To communicate with you about your account (e.g. email confirmation, password resets).
  • To understand usage patterns and improve the product.
  • To diagnose and fix bugs.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use your trip or packing data for advertising.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the app, and they only receive what's necessary to do their job:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles authentication.
  • Google — provides optional "Sign in with Google" and website analytics.
  • Open-Meteo — provides weather forecasts for trip destinations.
  • Vercel — hosts the website and app backend.

Content you add to a shared trip (like your packing list or trip details) is visible to the other Packmates on that trip — that's how the collaboration feature works. We otherwise don't share your personal information with third parties, except if required by law.

Your choices

You can review and edit your profile information at any time from the Profile tab. You can remove yourself from a trip, or delete a trip you created. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted entirely, email us at packmatesai@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.

Data retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain it for legal or security reasons.

Security

We rely on industry-standard practices from our infrastructure providers (encrypted connections, access controls, database-level row security) to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access.

Children's privacy

Packmates AI is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, let you know in the app.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email packmatesai@gmail.com.
