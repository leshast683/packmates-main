Japan Packing List: What to Actually Pack

Japan means a lot of walking and trains, cities that run cooler and rainier than you'd expect outside summer, and an economy that still leans heavily on cash.

Japan spans a huge range of climates and seasons. This list assumes a spring trip (mild temperatures, some rain — think cherry blossom season); shift it warmer for a July/August trip or add real cold-weather layers for December–February. Most first-time itineraries also mix in a temple-trail walk or two (Fushimi Inari, Arashiyama, Kumano Kodo), plus at least one proper sit-down dinner. Tokyo itself can run warm and humid with frequent rain outside winter, which is exactly the kind of forecast that shapes what Packmates AI actually suggests you pack.
