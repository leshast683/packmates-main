Costa Rica Packing List: What to Actually Pack

Costa Rica mixes rainforest, volcano, and beach in the same trip more often than not — pack quick-dry clothing, real rain gear even in "dry" season, and sun protection that survives sweat and humidity.

Even Costa Rica's official dry season sees regular afternoon showers, especially in rainforest and cloud-forest regions — a real rain jacket matters more than an umbrella here, since you'll likely be moving through trails or on a zip-line platform when it hits. Cotton takes forever to dry in this humidity; quick-dry fabrics make a bigger difference than people expect.
