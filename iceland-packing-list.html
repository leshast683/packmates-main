Iceland Packing List: What to Actually Pack

Iceland is cold, wet, and windy in almost every season — the layering system matters more here than the exact temperature on any given day.

Iceland's weather can shift dramatically within the same day, even in summer. A proper waterproof (not just water-resistant) outer layer matters more than sheer warmth — most of the discomfort on a Ring Road trip comes from wind and rain, not cold alone.
