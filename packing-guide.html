🏖️ Beach & Tropical Trips

Beyond the swimsuit — the things people actually forget are sun protection and something to protect your phone once you're in the water.

Swimsuit Beach towel Sunglasses Sun hat Sunscreen Aloe vera gel Flip flops Beach bag Waterproof phone case Snorkel set

💼 Business Trips

Pack for the meeting, not the vacation — and always bring one backup outfit in case of spills or a longer trip than planned.

Dress shirts Dress pants Blazer Dress shoes Tie Business cards Notebook Laptop & charger

🥾 Hiking & Outdoor Trips

Layers matter more than any single item — weather changes fast on trail, and a first aid kit is non-negotiable once you're more than an hour from a car.

Hiking boots Hiking socks Backpack Rain jacket Water bottle Hydration pack Insect repellent First aid kit Snacks Sunscreen

🎿 Ski & Cold-Weather Trips

The layer system: a thermal base layer, an insulating mid layer, then a waterproof shell. Most people under-pack socks and gloves specifically.

Thermal base layer Ski jacket Ski pants Gloves Ski goggles Beanie Wool socks Ski boots Hand warmers Heavy coat

🏙️ City Sightseeing Trips

Comfortable shoes matter more than anything else on this list — you'll walk far more than you expect. Everything else is about staying charged and dry.

Comfortable sneakers Crossbody bag or backpack Portable phone charger Travel guidebook or offline maps Compact umbrella Reusable water bottle Camera Sunglasses

🎒 Universal Essentials

Regardless of destination or trip type, these are the items worth double-checking before you leave the house.

Passport / ID Cash & cards Phone & charger Power bank Travel adapter Toothbrush & toothpaste Medications Underwear & socks
