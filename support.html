How does the AI packing list work?
When you create a trip, Packmates AI looks at your destination, dates, and the activities you select, then pulls in the real weather forecast for that trip to generate a packing checklist from our library of 250+ items — so you get a list built for a beach trip, not a generic one that also tells you to pack a swimsuit for a ski trip.
How do I share a trip with friends?
Open a trip and tap Share, or invite people as "Packmates" from your Profile. Everyone you invite can see and check off items on the shared packing list in real time.
Is Packmates AI free?
Yes — creating trips, generating packing lists, and collaborating with your travel crew is free.
Where do the airline baggage rules come from?
When you search for an airline while planning a trip, we show that airline's published carry-on and checked-bag size, weight, and fee guidelines. Airline policies change, so always double-check with your airline before you fly.
How do I delete my account or data?
Email packmatesai@gmail.com and we'll delete your account and associated data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Something's broken — what do I do?
Email us at packmatesai@gmail.com with what happened and, if you can, a screenshot — that helps us track it down fast.
