What is a packing assistant?

A packing assistant builds your packing list for you instead of leaving you staring at a blank page the night before a trip. Rather than a static template or a chatbot improvising an answer, Packmates AI works from your actual destination, travel dates, real weather, and planned activities — then keeps everyone traveling with you on the same live list.
