Las Vegas Packing List: What to Actually Pack

Vegas swings between two extremes on the same day — desert heat outside, aggressively cold AC inside every casino and club — and most trips mix pool days with real night-out dress codes. Pack for both.

Casino floors and clubs run their AC cold enough that a light layer isn't optional, even in summer — a cardigan or jacket you can throw on indoors makes a real difference. Most nightclubs and upscale restaurants also enforce dress codes (no sneakers, no shorts, no flip-flops for men in particular), so pack at least one outfit built around that before you land.
