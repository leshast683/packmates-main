Carry-On Only: The One-Bag Packing List

Packing carry-on-only comes down to one rule: everything has to earn its space. Here's what actually makes the cut versus what people bring "just in case" and never touch.

The real skill in carry-on packing isn't fitting more in — it's cutting the "just in case" items. This is a deliberately lean capsule: everything on it should be worn or used more than once. Add back destination-specific extras (swimwear, a rain layer, hiking boots) only if your actual trip needs them.

👕 The Core Capsule (5–7 days)

3–4 tops that mix and match 1–2 bottoms in a neutral color 1 layer (cardigan or light jacket) 1 pair of shoes you'll actually wear all trip Sleepwear Underwear & socks for each day (or plan to hand-wash) 1 "nice" outfit that also works casual

🧴 Toiletries (Travel-Size Only)

Toothbrush & toothpaste Travel-size shampoo & conditioner Deodorant Skincare in 3.4oz/100ml containers Contact lenses/glasses Any daily medication in original packaging

🎒 What Actually Justifies Its Space

Phone & charger A portable charger One book or a loaded e-reader Reusable water bottle (empty through security) Packing cubes (they're the real space-saver, not vacuum bags) A laundry bag or extra tote for dirty clothes on the way back
