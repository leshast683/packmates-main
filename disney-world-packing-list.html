Disney World Packing List: What to Actually Pack

A Disney park day means hours of walking and standing in Florida heat — comfortable broken-in shoes and a portable charger matter more than almost anything else you'll pack.

Park days routinely rack up 8–15,000+ steps in direct sun. Comfortable, already-broken-in shoes and a portable charger for your phone (it'll be running the park app all day) make a bigger difference than most people expect before their first visit. Factor in the resort pool for the evening, and one character-dining outfit if you've booked a reservation.
