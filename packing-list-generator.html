Common questions

Is the packing list generator really free, with no signup?

Yes. Enter your destination, dates, and activities and the list generates instantly in your browser - no account, no email, nothing saved anywhere unless you choose to create a free Packmates AI account afterward.

How does the packing list generator decide what to include?

It uses the same logic as the Packmates AI app: your destination and travel month set an expected weather profile, and the activities you pick (beach, hiking, business, baby, and more) pull in matching items from a database of 250+ packing items.

Can I save or edit the generated packing list?

The generator gives you an instant preview with no account needed. To save a list, check items off as you pack, or share it with people you're traveling with, create a free Packmates AI account and it becomes a real trip you can keep updating.

Does the generated list change if I add more activities?

Yes - add or remove activities and regenerate to see the list update immediately. In the full Packmates AI app, this same adaptive behavior keeps working after you've saved the trip, as your plans or the weather forecast change.
