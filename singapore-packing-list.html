Singapore Packing FAQ

What clothes should I pack for Singapore?

Light, breathable clothes — it's hot and humid all year, in every month. Natural or quick-dry fabrics, shorts and light trousers, and clothes that still look fine after a humid day. Then add the one thing visitors forget: a light layer for indoors, because malls, trains and restaurants run their air-conditioning cold.

Does it rain much in Singapore?

Yes — sudden, heavy downpours are a normal part of the year, and more frequent in the wetter months around November to January. A compact umbrella or light rain jacket and shoes that can get wet cover it; storms are intense but usually pass quickly, and the city is built to keep you under cover between them.

What am I not allowed to bring into Singapore?

Leave vapes and e-cigarettes at home — they're prohibited, and bringing one in is an offence. Chewing gum is also banned from import and sale, so don't pack it. And there's no duty-free allowance for cigarettes: any you bring must be declared and taxed on arrival. Check the official customs list before you fly if you carry medication or anything unusual.

Do I need smart clothes for Singapore?

Not much. Singapore is practical: hawker centres and most sightseeing are fully casual. One smart-casual outfit covers nicer restaurants and bars. In Packmates AI, a Singapore list stays short — the win is adding the AC layer and rain cover, not more outfits.

What shoes are best for Singapore?

Comfortable walking shoes or sturdy sandals that can survive a downpour. You'll walk a lot between sights, stations and food courts, often in heat and sometimes in sudden rain. One pair of breathable walking shoes plus sandals is the standard local answer; leave anything heavy or slow-drying at home.
